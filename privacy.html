Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026  |  Sociomonkey AI Pvt. Ltd.

1. Who We Are

Sociomonkey (app.sociomonkey.com) is a Lead Management System (LMS) that helps real estate businesses capture, manage, and convert leads from digital advertising platforms including Meta (Facebook / Instagram) and Google Ads.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: Name, email address, and password when you register.
  • Lead data: Contact information submitted by end-users through Meta Lead Ads or Google Ads Lead Form extensions that you (the tenant) have connected to Sociomonkey.
  • Facebook / Meta data: When you authorise Sociomonkey via Facebook Login, we receive an access token, your Facebook user ID, name, and a list of Pages you manage. We use this solely to retrieve lead form submissions on your behalf.
  • Google data: When you authorise Sociomonkey via Google OAuth, we receive an access token and refresh token scoped to Google Ads. We use this solely to retrieve lead form submissions on your behalf.
  • Usage data: IP address, browser type, and pages visited for security and analytics purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver leads from connected advertising platforms into your LMS dashboard in real time.
  • To authenticate your account and maintain your session.
  • To send transactional notifications (e.g. new lead alerts) where enabled.
  • To improve the reliability and performance of the platform.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

4. Facebook / Meta Data Usage

Sociomonkey requests the following Meta permissions on your behalf:

  • leads_retrieval – to read lead submissions from your Facebook Lead Ads forms.
  • pages_manage_ads – to list lead forms attached to your Facebook Pages.
  • pages_read_engagement – to list the Facebook Pages you manage.

Data obtained through these permissions is used exclusively to populate your LMS and is never shared with third parties. You can revoke access at any time from your Facebook App Settings.

5. Data Retention

Lead data is retained as long as your account is active or as required to provide the service. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

6. Data Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access tokens are stored encrypted. We follow industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

7. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services: Vercel (hosting), Supabase / PostgreSQL (database), Brevo (transactional email). Each operates under their own privacy policy.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, email us at privacy@sociomonkey.ai.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
