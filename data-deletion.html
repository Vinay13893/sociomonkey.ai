Data Deletion Instructions

Emount Ventures Private Limited  |  Sociomonkey

Sociomonkey respects user privacy and provides users with the ability to request deletion of their personal data.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your account or personal data associated with Sociomonkey, please send an email to:

aseem@sociomonkey.com

What to Include in Your Request

  • Your full name
  • Organization name
  • Registered email address
  • Description of the deletion request

Processing Timeline

Upon verification of the request, Sociomonkey will process the deletion within 30 days, subject to any legal or contractual obligations that require data retention.

Third-Party Integrations

If you have connected third-party services such as Meta (Facebook), Google, WhatsApp, or other integrations, associated tokens and connection records will also be removed as part of the deletion process.

Contact

For questions regarding privacy or data handling, please contact:
aseem@sociomonkey.com
